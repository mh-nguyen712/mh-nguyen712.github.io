[DeepInverse](https://deepinv.github.io/deepinv/) covers every step of image reconstruction: efficient forward operators
(optics, MRI, tomography, ...), variational problems and their solvers, and the design and training of neural network architectures.
It joined the [PyTorch Ecosystem](https://pytorch.org/blog/deepinverse-joins-pytorch-ecosystem/) in 2025 and received the
French National Prize for Open Science Software in 2024.

I contribute to the API design, benchmarks, CI/CD, packaging, documentation and community support.
