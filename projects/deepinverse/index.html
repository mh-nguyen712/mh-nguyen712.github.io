DeepInverse

Core contributor and maintainer · 2023 – present

Open-source PyTorch library for solving imaging inverse problems with deep learning, part of the PyTorch Ecosystem.

Website GitHub Paper

DeepInverse covers every step of image reconstruction: efficient forward operators (optics, MRI, tomography, …), variational problems and their solvers, and the design and training of neural network architectures. It joined the PyTorch Ecosystem in 2025 and received the French National Prize for Open Science Software in 2024.

I contribute to the API design, benchmarks, CI/CD, packaging, documentation and community support.
