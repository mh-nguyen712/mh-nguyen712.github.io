DeepVibes

Research engineer, Agenium Space · 2022 – 2023

Estimating and correcting micro-vibrations in satellite images acquired with pushbroom cameras, validated on Sentinel-2B data.

Paper Interactive demo

Pushbroom cameras build an image line by line as the satellite moves along its orbit. Tiny oscillations of the satellite’s attitude during acquisition, the micro-vibrations, make each line look in a slightly different direction: straight roads become wavy and the spectral bands, which sit at different places on the focal plane, no longer line up.

DeepVibes models the acquisition process with the satellite attitude as a parameter, then corrects the images in two stages: an identification network estimates the attitude, and a deep unrolled network uses it to reconstruct the image. We found that the geometry of the focal plane plays a key role in making the vibrations identifiable.
